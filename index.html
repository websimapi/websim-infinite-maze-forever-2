How to Play

Explore an infinite maze that you and others create together!

  • Movement:
    • Desktop: WASD or Arrow Keys. Click canvas to lock mouse for look-based movement.
    • Mobile: Use the joystick that appears in the bottom-left.
  • Drawing: Walk into an empty black/patterned square to claim it and start drawing. Use the palette, brush, and Undo tools that appear. Click 'Finish Drawing' when done. Your drawing will be checked by AI for appropriateness before being saved.
  • Importing Images: While drawing, click 'Import Img' to select an image file. It will be scaled and converted to the available palette colors. Dithering can be toggled in Settings.
  • Colors: Light colors are floors (you can walk on them), dark colors are walls (you can't walk through them).
  • Zoom: Use the mouse wheel (desktop) or the +/- buttons (mobile) to zoom.
  • Chat: Use the input box below the messages to chat. Click the 💬 button next to your coordinates to toggle the chat window.
  • 3D Mode: Toggle 3D Mode in Settings to explore the maze in first-person view. In 3D Mode, use the mouse to look and WASD/arrow keys to move in the direction you're facing.
  • Other Buttons: ⚙️ (Settings) adjusts controls. 📊 (Stats) shows maze info and past maps. ? (Info) shows this help. 🔄 (Reset) casts your vote to reset the entire map. If 60% of players vote, the map resets! 💾 (Save) saves an image of the maze.

Tip: You can only enter squares that are truly empty (black/patterned). You cannot enter squares currently being drawn by others (indicated by a rainbow overlay and username). If a square looks empty but you can't enter, the map might be syncing - try again shortly!

Saving Images: Use the 💾 button to save an image. You can choose to save just the square you are currently in, or the entire discovered map. Large maps may take time to generate!

Content Moderation: Drawings are automatically checked by an AI for inappropriate content (NSFW, hate speech, etc.). Rejected drawings will not be saved. Moderation occurs both when finishing a single square and across adjacent squares by the same user.
